# PRIVACY POLICY & POPIA COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Platform:** ALIS (Automated Legal Intelligence System)  
**Regulatory Framework:** Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA)  

---

## 1. Commitment to Privacy
ALIS respects your constitutional right to privacy and is fully committed to complying with the Protection of Information Act (POPIA). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and secure your personal and legal document data.

## 2. Collection of Personal Information
In operating our hyperlocal LegalTech and document intelligence workflows, we may collect:
* Contact details (Full name, email address, phone number).
* Uploaded legal documents, contracts, case briefs, and evidence logs submitted for automated analysis.
* Technical telemetry data, IP addresses, and device identifiers required for secure session authentication.

## 3. Purpose of Processing (POPIA Section 13)
Personal and document information is processed strictly for:
* Providing automated document intelligence, summarization, and case preparation features.
* Facilitating secure consultations and communication.
* Complying with statutory obligations under South African law.

## 4. Data Security & Confidentiality
* **Encryption:** All data in transit is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards.
* **Access Control:** Strict role-based access control (RBAC) ensures that only authorized system processes and the account holder can inspect sensitive legal file payloads.

## 5. Data Subject Rights (POPIA Section 5)
Under POPIA, you retain the right to:
* Request access to your personal information held by ALIS.
* Request correction, destruction, or deletion of your personal records.
* Object to the processing of your personal information by contacting our Information Officer.
